How to play ?
  • The goal is easy: buy the best die
  • You start with 3 dice: 0;1 and 2
  • You will keep 0 all the game
  • The max number is 6 dice
  • You will buy better dice in game ex 0;1;2;3;4;5 or 0;25;26;27;28;29
  • You roll a die and earn the number in points
  • You can choose if you want to:
    • Convert in xp and earn the middle in $
    • Try to have better score
  • ⚠️ If you got a 0 you loose all your points not converts ⚠️
  • If you got 5x a number other than 0 you earn +0.1x points for each die after
  • You can buy many boosts in the shop !
